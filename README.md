One page that catches the world on Tuesday 24 November 2026: a broadsheet front page built from the day's reporting. The lead story is the Strait of Hormuz, where Iran said four tankers struck naval mines and caught fire and its Revolutionary Guard said its navy hit a container ship. Around it, five short reports: a US jury convicting Huawei of trade-secret theft and bank fraud; Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian ships in the Gulf of Finland and off Libya; Houthi fighters 60 km from Aden and a suspected drone strike on Riyadh's tallest tower; China launching Shenzhou 24 with the first Pakistani astronaut bound for Tiangong; and a Turkish Air Force helicopter crash that killed five. The globe illustration supplied with the entry anchors the top of the page.

The page is drawn by `main.py`, which needs nothing beyond Python's standard library. It writes the finished 1024×1024 `entry.png` into `/output` by default; point `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` somewhere else to change that. The illustration it embeds lives in `assets/hero.png`.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
